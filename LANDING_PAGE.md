## ✨ What You Can Do

### **As a Customer**
- 🔍 Browse and search for home chefs
- 🍽️ Explore food items and menus
- ⭐ View chef ratings and reviews
- 📝 Place orders and track delivery
- ❤️ Save favorite chefs and dishes

### **As a Chef**
- 👨‍🍳 Manage your chef profile
- 🍲 Add and update food items
- 📊 Track orders and earnings
- ⭐ View customer reviews
- 🚀 Set availability and pricing

### **As an Admin**
- 👥 Manage all users
- 📈 Monitor orders and payments
- 🔍 Handle customer support
- 💳 Process refunds
- 📊 View analytics

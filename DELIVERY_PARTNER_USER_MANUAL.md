## Getting Started

Welcome to HomeChefs Delivery! This manual will guide you through the complete process of becoming a delivery partner and using our platform to earn money by delivering homemade food to customers.

### What You'll Need
- Valid driving license
- Registered vehicle (bike, scooter, car, or bicycle)
- Smartphone with internet access
- Bank account for payments
- Government ID for verification

---

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Access the Registration Page
1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `http://127.0.0.1:8000/delivery/register/`
3. You'll see the "Register as Delivery Partner" form

### Step 2: Fill Account Information
**Required Fields:**
- **Username**: Choose a unique username (no spaces)
- **Email**: Your active email address
- **First Name**: Your legal first name
- **Last Name**: Your legal last name
- **Password**: Create a strong password (min 8 characters)
- **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

### Step 3: Contact Information
**Required Fields:**
- **Phone Number**: Include country code (e.g., +1234567890)
- **Address**: Your current residential address

### Step 4: Vehicle Information
**Required Fields:**
- **Vehicle Type**: Select from:
  - Bike
  - Scooter  
  - Car
  - Bicycle
- **Vehicle Number**: Your vehicle's registration number
- **License Number**: Your valid driving license number

### Step 5: Service Area Configuration
**Required Fields:**
- **Service Areas**: List areas you can deliver to (comma-separated)
  - Example: "Downtown, Uptown, Suburbia, North Side"
- **Max Delivery Distance**: Maximum distance you're willing to travel (1-50 km)
- **Profile Picture**: Optional but recommended

### Step 6: Terms and Conditions
- Read the terms and conditions carefully
- Check the box to agree to the terms
- Click "Register as Delivery Partner"

### Step 7: Registration Success
After successful registration:
- You'll see a success message
- Your account is now pending admin verification
- You'll receive an email once verified

## Dashboard Overview

Once your account is verified and logged in, you'll see the Delivery Dashboard:

### Main Sections

#### 1. Statistics Cards (Top)
- **Active**: Number of current deliveries
- **Today**: Completed deliveries today
- **Pending**: Delivery requests waiting for response
- **Earnings**: Today's total earnings

#### 2. Availability Toggle
- Switch to toggle your availability ON/OFF
- When OFF, you won't receive delivery requests
- When ON, you'll receive requests from your service area

#### 3. Pending Delivery Requests
- Shows active delivery requests in your area
- Each request displays:
  - Order ID and meal details
  - Customer information
  - Distance and delivery fee
  - Time remaining to accept/decline

#### 4. Active Deliveries
- Shows currently accepted deliveries
- Each delivery displays:
  - Order status and details
  - Action buttons for status updates
  - Delivery progress

## Managing Delivery Requests

### Receiving Requests
When a food order is ready for delivery in your service area:
1. You'll see a new request in the "Pending Delivery Requests" section
2. Each request shows a countdown timer (5 minutes to respond)
3. You can choose to Accept or Decline

### Accepting a Request
1. Click the green **"Accept"** button
2. The system will:
   - Assign the delivery to you
   - Update your availability to OFF
   - Show delivery details
   - Mark the order as "Out for Delivery"

### Declining a Request
1. Click the red **"Decline"** button
2. The request will be offered to other partners
3. Your availability remains unchanged

### Request Details
Each request includes:
- **Order ID**: Unique order identifier
- **Meal Name**: What food needs to be delivered
- **Portions**: Number of portions
- **Customer Name**: Who you're delivering to
- **Distance**: How far the delivery is
- **Delivery Fee**: How much you'll earn
- **Expires In**: Time left to respond

## Delivery Process

### Step 1: View Delivery Details
After accepting a request:
1. Click the **"Details"** button
2. Review pickup and delivery information

### Pickup Information
- **Chef Name**: Who prepared the food
- **Chef Phone**: Contact number for pickup
- **Pickup Address**: Where to collect the food
- **Order Details**: What you're picking up

### Delivery Information
- **Customer Name**: Who you're delivering to
- **Customer Phone**: Contact number for delivery
- **Delivery Address**: Where to deliver
- **Special Instructions**: Any customer notes

### Step 2: Mark as Picked Up
1. Go to the pickup location
2. Collect the food from the chef
3. Verify the order details
4. Click **"Picked Up"** button
5. Order status changes to "Picked Up"

### Step 3: Mark as In Transit
1. Start traveling to customer location
2. Click **"In Transit"** button
3. Order status changes to "In Transit"
4. Customer receives notification that you're on the way

### Step 4: Complete Delivery
1. Arrive at delivery address
2. Contact customer if needed
3. Hand over the food
4. Click **"Delivered"** button
5. Add delivery notes (optional)
6. Order status changes to "Delivered"
7. Your availability turns back ON
8. Earnings are added to your account

## Earnings and Statistics

### Viewing Your Statistics
1. Click **"Statistics"** in the sidebar
2. View comprehensive performance data

### Available Metrics
- **Total Deliveries**: All-time completed deliveries
- **Completed Deliveries**: Successfully finished orders
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of accepted deliveries completed
- **Average Rating**: Customer ratings (1-5 stars)
- **Total Earnings**: All-time earnings
- **Today's Deliveries**: Deliveries completed today
- **Today's Earnings**: Money earned today
- **Monthly Stats**: Current month performance

### Earnings Calculation
- **Delivery Fee**: Based on distance (base fee + per km rate)
- **Your Earnings**: 80% of the delivery fee
- **Platform Fee**: 20% goes to platform maintenance

### Payment Process
- Earnings are calculated automatically
- Payments are processed weekly/monthly
- Bank transfer to registered account

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Log In
- Check username and password
- Ensure account is verified
- Try password reset if needed

#### No Delivery Requests
- Check your availability is ON
- Verify your service areas are correct
- Ensure you're in an active delivery zone

#### Request Expired
- Requests expire after 5 minutes
- Respond quickly to new requests
- Check internet connection

#### Can't Update Status
- Ensure you're connected to internet
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if issue persists

#### Location Issues
- Verify pickup/delivery addresses
- Use GPS for navigation
- Call customer/chef if needed

### Getting Help
1. Check this manual first
2. Use the in-app help section
3. Contact support at: support@homechefs.com
4. Call hotline: 1-800-HOMECHEFS

## Best Practices

### For Maximum Earnings
1. **Stay Available**: Keep availability ON during peak hours
2. **Quick Responses**: Accept requests within 2 minutes
3. **Service Areas**: Choose busy areas with high demand
4. **Peak Hours**: Be active during lunch (11 AM-2 PM) and dinner (6 PM-9 PM)

### For Customer Satisfaction
1. **Be Professional**: Maintain professional appearance and attitude
2. **Communication**: Call customers if running late
3. **Food Safety**: Handle food carefully and maintain temperature
4. **Navigation**: Use GPS for efficient routes
5. **Verification**: Confirm customer identity before handing over food

### For Safety
1. **Vehicle Safety**: Follow traffic rules and wear helmet
2. **Personal Safety**: Share location with family/friends
3. **Food Safety**: Keep food secure and covered
4. **Emergency Contacts**: Keep emergency numbers handy

### Time Management
1. **Route Planning**: Plan efficient delivery routes
2. **Peak Hours**: Understand your area's busy times
3. **Multiple Orders**: Handle multiple deliveries efficiently
4. **Breaks**: Take regular breaks to avoid fatigue

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How long does verification take?**
A: Usually 24-48 hours after registration.

**Q: What if I can't find the pickup location?**
A: Call the chef using the provided phone number.

**Q: Can I decline delivery requests?**
A: Yes, but frequent declines may affect your rating.

**Q: How are delivery fees calculated?**
A: Based on distance - base fee + per km rate.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Weekly transfers to your registered bank account.

**Q: What if customer doesn't answer?**
A: Wait 5 minutes, then call again. If no response, contact support.

**Q: Can I deliver in multiple cities?**
A: Yes, add all areas to your service areas list.

**Q: What if my vehicle breaks down?**
A: Contact support immediately and inform about the situation.
